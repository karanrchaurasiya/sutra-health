Understanding Integrative Medicine

What Is Integrative Medicine?

Integrative medicine is a whole-person approach to healthcare that considers appropriate conventional and complementary approaches in a coordinated way.

Rather than focusing only on one symptom or one part of the body, an integrative approach can consider the person’s health, lifestyle, behaviours, environment and individual circumstances.

The specific approaches used should depend on the person, their health needs, safety considerations and appropriate professional guidance.

Important Distinction

Integrative vs Complementary vs Alternative Medicine

These terms are related but should not be treated as interchangeable. Understanding the distinction helps people make more informed healthcare decisions.

01

Complementary

An approach used alongside conventional healthcare.

02

Alternative

An approach used instead of conventional healthcare.

03

Integrative

A coordinated approach that considers appropriate conventional and complementary care within a whole-person framework.

Important

Complementary approaches should not be used as a reason to delay appropriate medical evaluation or replace prescribed care without qualified professional guidance.
