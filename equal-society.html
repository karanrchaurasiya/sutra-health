About Us

About EQUAL Society — Faridabad's Chronic Disease Reversal NGO

Effort For Quality of Life (EQUAL) Society, also known as Life Quality / Sutra Health, is a Faridabad-based not-for-profit organization committed to the attainment of the highest quality of life for all through natural, evidence-based chronic disease reversal.

Registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860 in Haryana vide no. 363, dated 28.5.1997, and recognized under Sections 12A and 80G of the Income Tax Act — donations qualify for tax exemption.

We believe in the holistic development of individuals and communities through integrative health (Yoga therapy, Ayurveda, nutrition and modern medicine), education, empowerment, awareness and sustainable living — helping people in Faridabad and across India reverse conditions like diabetes, hypertension, obesity and joint pain.
