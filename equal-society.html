About EQUAL Society

Effort For Quality of Life

Effort For Quality of Life (EQUAL) Society, also known as Life Quality / Sutra Health, is a Faridabad-based not-for-profit organization committed to the attainment of the highest quality of life for all through natural, evidence-based chronic disease reversal.

Registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860 in Haryana vide no. 363, dated 28.5.1997, and recognized under Sections 12A and 80G of the Income Tax Act.

The Society works toward holistic development through integrative health, education, empowerment, awareness and sustainable living, with work spanning Yoga therapy, Ayurveda, nutrition and appropriate modern healthcare.

1997 Founded in Haryana

A long-standing not-for-profit initiative focused on quality of life and community wellbeing.
