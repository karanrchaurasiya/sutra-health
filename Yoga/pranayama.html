Understanding Pranayama

What Is Pranayama?

Pranayama is a traditional yogic practice based on conscious awareness and regulation of the breath. These pranayama breathing exercises use different patterns of inhalation, exhalation and breath control. Different pranayama techniques use different patterns of inhalation, exhalation and breath control.

Breathing practices are traditionally included in yoga, meditation and holistic wellness routines. Depending on the technique, pranayama may be practiced to develop breathing awareness, relaxation, concentration and mindfulness.

This Pranayama Protocol introduces eight traditional practices: Kapalbhati, Anulom Vilom, Nadi Shodhan, Bhastrika, Ujjayi, Sheetali-Sheetkari, Bhramari and Omkar.

07

Bhramari

A humming-based breathing practice traditionally used for relaxation and inward attention.

08

Omkar

A traditional practice combining conscious breathing with vocalization of the Om sound.

Pranayama & Wellness

Pranayama Benefits and Health

Pranayama is commonly practiced as part of a broader yoga and healthy-lifestyle routine.

Stress & Relaxation

Slow, controlled breathing practices may be included in relaxation, mindfulness and stress-management routines.

Yoga & Lifestyle

Pranayama can be practiced alongside yoga, meditation, physical activity and other healthy lifestyle habits.

Diabetes & Wellness

Pranayama and diabetes are sometimes discussed together within broader yoga and lifestyle approaches. Breathing practices should complement, not replace, appropriate diabetes care.

Individual Practice

The choice and intensity of a breathing practice should consider experience, comfort and individual circumstances.

Important

Pranayama is a complementary wellness practice and should not replace prescribed medication, diagnosis or medical treatment. Practice comfortably and stop if you experience dizziness, discomfort or unusual symptoms.
